# Deadlines

Everything graded in this course: homework, tests (kolokwium, sprawdzian), labs and the exam.
`study gui` ranks open rows by points per remaining day ("Points at stake"), and a test or exam within
14 days (homework 7, lab 3) puts this subject in focus: the other subjects wait.

- **Kind:** exam · test · homework · lab
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD (blank if not known yet)
- **Points:** the most you can get · **Got:** what you got (filled in = done) · **Need:** the pass mark
