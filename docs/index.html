What it does

📚 Teaches from your materials

Every explanation cites your own files, like (Lecture3.pdf p.12) or (Lecture5.mp4 [00:14:30]). No answers from thin air.

🧠 Remembers what you forgot

Spaced repetition per topic (1 → 3 → 7 → 16 → 35 days) and a log of every mistake, re-tested until it is fixed.

✍️ Tutors, doesn't lecture

One idea at a time (≤ 150 words), then a 2–5 minute task. Hints before answers, honest grading.

📝 Mock exams in your format

Quizzes and full mock exams that match your course's real exam, with a weak-spot report and revision plan.

🎥 Transcribes lectures locally

Video and audio become timestamped text with whisper.cpp, on your machine. Paste a lecture link and it downloads it.

🔎 Finds things by meaning

Optional offline semantic search: a question in one language finds the slide in another. Scanned PDFs are read with OCR.

Who it is for

University and college students who learn from their own course materials and want an AI tutor that is grounded in those materials rather than the open web — especially anyone revising for exams, juggling several courses, or working with lecture recordings and scanned notes. It is a local-first, open-source alternative to pasting slides into a general chatbot, and a more active companion than a flashcard deck.

Quick start

git clone https://github.com/dymchenkko/claude-study-agent.git ~/Study
cd ~/Study && claude          # then type /setup
  1. /setup checks your machine, installs what is missing and creates your first subject.
  2. Drop files into <Subject>/materials/ — slides, PDFs, videos, notes.
  3. Type /ingest <Subject>. It converts everything and builds a topic map.
  4. Type /today every day. That's it.

No files yet? /learn Example-Subject osmosis uses the included demo course.

FAQ

What is Claude Study Agent?

An open-source AI tutor for university courses, built as a set of Claude Code skills. You put your lecture slides, PDFs and recordings in a folder; it converts them to searchable text, teaches you topic by topic with short tasks and citations, schedules reviews with spaced repetition, logs your mistakes and builds mock exams in your course's exam format.

How is it different from NotebookLM, ChatGPT or Anki?

Unlike a general chatbot, every answer is grounded in your own course materials with page citations. Unlike a flashcard app like Anki, it writes the questions for you and remembers why you got something wrong. It adds spaced repetition, mock exams in your real exam format, and local transcription and search of lecture recordings.

Do I need an API key? What does it cost?

No API key. It runs on your existing Claude Pro or Max subscription through Claude Code. Conversion, OCR, transcription and semantic search are free, open-source and run on your own machine.

Do my lecture files get uploaded anywhere?

Your files stay on your computer. Conversion, OCR, transcription and the search index all run locally. Only the passages the tutor reads while teaching are sent to Claude, like any Claude Code session.

Which file types and languages does it support?

PDF (including scanned PDFs via OCR), PPTX, DOCX, XLSX, Markdown and text, plus video and audio (MP4, MOV, MKV, WEBM, M4A, MP3, WAV) and lecture links. Materials can be in any language Claude reads, and semantic search works across languages.

Does it work on Windows?

Yes, through WSL (Windows Subsystem for Linux). Native Windows is not supported.
